What makes it different

Built for real budgets,
real families

Every feature in Budget Ease is designed around one idea: you should always know exactly how much is safe to spend.

Envelope Budgeting

Organize your money into Pockets — spending categories and savings goals — so every dollar has a purpose before you spend it.

Safe to Spend

Your personal financial north star. Always visible on the dashboard, always up to date. Know what you can actually spend right now.

Offline First

No internet connection needed. No account to create. Your data lives on your device — private, fast, and always available.

English & Spanish

Fully bilingual. Switch between English and Spanish at any time in Settings — designed for families across Latin America and beyond.

Support

Common questions

Everything you need to get started with Budget Ease.

Yes — Budget Ease is completely free to download and use. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no hidden fees. All features are available from the moment you open the app.
No account required. Budget Ease is fully offline — your data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server, and no personal information is collected.
Open the Pocket you want to reset, tap the options menu, and select "Reset Pocket." This will clear all transactions in that spending pocket and restore its full budget. Note: savings pockets cannot be reset — their balance carries over indefinitely.
Your dashboard shows two numbers. Available Money is everything you have minus what's set aside in savings goals. Safe to Spend goes one step further and also subtracts what's left in your spending pockets — it's the money that isn't budgeted for anything yet, so you can spend it without touching a budget or a goal.
Yes! Budget Ease is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store, and for Android on Google Play.
Go to Settings (the gear icon) and select your preferred language. Budget Ease is fully available in both English and Spanish, with all screens and labels translated.
