Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Budget Ease does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your data never leaves your device.

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Pocket Sprout ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Budget Ease mobile application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. Budget Ease is designed from the ground up to be a fully private, offline-first experience.

Information We Do Not Collect

Budget Ease does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Financial data, transaction history, or budget information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to our servers

How Your Data Is Stored

All data you enter into Budget Ease — including your pockets, transactions, and balances — is stored locally on your device using your device's built-in storage. This data is never transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

We have no access to your financial data at any time.

No Account Required

Budget Ease does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use any feature of the app. The app is fully functional from the moment you install it.

Third-Party Services

Budget Ease does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No third-party SDKs that collect data are included in the app.

Children's Privacy

Because Budget Ease does not collect any personal information from any users, there are no special concerns regarding children's privacy. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Any changes will be posted here and, if significant, noted in the app's release notes.
